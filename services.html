Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our timelines, pricing structure, and warranties.

Our standard execution timeline is strictly 45 working days from design finalization to handover. For large multi-story villas (4BHK+), the timeline ranges between 60 to 75 days. You receive weekly milestone photo updates from our project manager.
No. UrbanNest operates on a 100% transparent pricing policy. We provide an itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) covering materials, hardware, electrical, paint, and labor before work begins. Unless you request layout changes midway, the final price remains fixed.
We offer up to a 10-year warranty on factory-manufactured woodwork, anti-termite and moisture-resistant BWP plywood, and lifetime warranties on premium soft-close fittings from certified partners like Hettich and Hafele. Plus, every project includes 1 year of free service support.
Absolutely! Our Anna Nagar studio houses physical catalogs and sample swatches of Italian laminates, acrylics, natural veneers, quartz surfaces, and fabrics. Sneha and Ananya will guide you through combinations that fit both your aesthetics and durability needs.
We believe in milestone-based fairness: 10% on booking & 3D finalization, 40% on procurement of factory-pressed boards, 40% on completion of woodwork installation on site, and the remaining 10% on final handover and sign-off.
