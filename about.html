Our Story

Crafting homes that echo your personality.

UrbanNest started in 2018 with two designers and one big belief: good design should feel personal, not generic. Today our team of 14 has completed 250+ projects across Chennai, Coimbatore, and Bengaluru.

From our studio on Lake View Road in Anna Nagar, we collaborate closely with homeowners, software engineers, doctors, and startup founders. We believe your living room should celebrate weekend conversations, and your kitchen should support real culinary joy.

Our Principles

How We Approach Every Room

Our values guide every decision, from initial 3D renderings to the final turn of a screwdriver.

🌱

Human-Centered Spaces

We design around how you live — where you charge your phone, how you brew your morning coffee, and where your family unwinds.

📏

Millimeter Precision
